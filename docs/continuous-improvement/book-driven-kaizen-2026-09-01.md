## Deliberate exclusions

- Vendor interface details and current plan limits from *Claude For Dummies* are
  not permanent doctrine; they are volatile and require current verification.
- AGI, AGE, synthetic empathy, and far-future embodiment arguments from *The AI
  Instinct* remain conceptual context, not product claims or autonomous-agent
  permissions.
- DeepSeek's MEAP is incomplete and explicitly does not cover production
  serving or safety filters. Its architectural trade-offs are references only;
  no model-performance or production-readiness claim was imported.
- Salesforce-specific tool names and platform limits were generalised into
  metadata-driven quality patterns rather than creating a Salesforce catalogue.
- The health-communication source's clinical, legal, and public-health claims
  remain subject to qualified review and current authoritative sources.
