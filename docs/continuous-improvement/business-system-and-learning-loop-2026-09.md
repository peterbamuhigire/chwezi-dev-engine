# Business-System and Learning-Loop Contract

Purpose: shared vocabulary for book-informed Kaizen work across the local engines.

## Decision frame

Every material recommendation records:

1. **Aim** — the decision or outcome, audience, time horizon, and boundary.
2. **Evidence** — observed facts, source IDs, assumptions, and uncertainty.
3. **System effects** — customer/beneficiary value, revenue or mission result, cash/cost, capacity, quality, resilience, adaptability, and stakeholder impact.
4. **Choice** — selected action, rejected alternatives, trade-off, owner, and decision threshold.
5. **Learning loop** — baseline, small reversible experiment, leading and lagging measures, guardrail, stop condition, rollback, and re-audit date.

Use the whole-system lens before optimising one dimension. A cheaper process may reduce quality or adaptability; a stronger brand may not produce cash; a faster agent may increase review or recovery burden. Record the interaction rather than hiding it in a single score.

## Improvement loop

`Observe -> Baseline -> Select -> Experiment -> Check -> Standardise -> Teach -> Re-measure`

The experiment record must say what would count as improvement, what would count as harm, how the prior baseline is restored, and who can stop the change. A green structural validator is not evidence of business, mission, safety, or user improvement.

## Replication and local adaptation

Separate the invariant core from the adaptable edge:

- invariant: promise, safety, accounting/control integrity, acceptance criteria, evidence rules, and minimum quality;
- adaptable: channel, language, local workflow, examples, sequencing, and operating detail;
- escalation: any proposed exception that could change the promise, risk, financial meaning, legal position, or evidence standard.

This applies to franchise-like operating systems, reusable delivery methods, templates, runbooks, and agent workflows.

## Learning transfer

Design learning as `explain -> demonstrate -> practise -> assess -> support -> observe transfer -> measure result`. Name the critical behaviour, the workplace driver or barrier, the leading indicator, the outcome measure, the owner, and the review cadence. Participant satisfaction alone is not transfer evidence.

## Human-agent orchestration

Before an agent acts, record the task boundary, decision owner, permitted tools, data scope, reversibility class, evidence required, review point, escalation route, kill/containment action, and recovery owner. Select autonomy deliberately; increase it only after evidence from lower-risk cases. After failure, record detection, containment, recovery, human decision, and system learning.

## Source and currentness boundary

Books support stable concepts, historical cases, and hypotheses. Digital Research controls source admission, currentness, claim support, and verification. For current standards, policies, laws, versions, APIs, security instructions, market data, or platform behaviour, use a primary source, record access and review dates, and mark unresolved claims `NOT ASSESSED`.
