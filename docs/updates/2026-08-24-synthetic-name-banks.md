# Synthetic name-bank standard

The SaaS seeding skills now include a portable four-CSV starter bank and a
deterministic selection contract for fictional human identities. The starter
bank covers English/western and Muslim/Arabic cohorts, keeps given-name gender
selection separate from surname tradition, requires a checksum, and rejects
duplicate or undersized pools.

The contract is documented at
`skills/saas/full-coverage-saas-seeding/references/synthetic-name-bank-standard.md`.
The `saas-seeder` skill points to the same standard for bootstrap and recurring
demo refresh work.
