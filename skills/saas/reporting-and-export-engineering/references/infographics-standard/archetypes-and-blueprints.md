# Archetypes and blueprints

Use this reference to pick the right infographic form for the message, then build it to its
contract. Choose by the relationship the reader must understand, never by a picture that looked good
elsewhere. Rules: [rules-and-gates.md](rules-and-gates.md).

## What the reference images teach

The handbook's reference set spans process maps, cycles, illustrated instructions, comparison posters,
isometric facilities, statistical dashboards, customer journeys and editorial spreads. They are
inspiration, never factual sources.

| Keep | Reject |
|---|---|
| Explicit sequence, distinct visual groups, a dominant title, a readable legend, careful rhythm, clear connectors, illustration that explains a mechanism | Gratuitous 3D, excess decorative icons, tiny labels, unrelated imagery, fabricated charts, typographic distortion, misleading perspective, duplicated steps, invented-looking claims |

## Choosing the archetype

| The reader must understand | Archetype | Structure check |
|---|---|---|
| How much, compared or over time | Statistical chart or small multiples | Lie factor, zero baseline, units, period |
| Where something is or varies by place | Map or geographic figure | Sourced current boundaries, legend, scale, rates not raw counts, projection |
| What happens in order | Process flow or instruction sequence | Graph check: unique steps, reachability, numbering |
| What repeats | Cycle or loop | Single closed ring, entry point, direction |
| When things happen | Timeline or roadmap | True time scale or "sequence, not to scale" |
| Which option fits | Comparison or decision matrix | Symmetric criteria, N/A shown |
| Who reports to whom, what contains what | Hierarchy or tree | One root, one parent each, no cycle |
| What a customer or service goes through | Journey, service blueprint, swimlanes | Actors, channels and touchpoints distinct |
| How a physical system works | Technical cutaway or isometric map | Expert-verified topology and labels |
| How to do something safely | Educational or safety poster | Authoritative guidance, qualified reviewer |
| A number in a feed | Social or mobile card | First-frame message, canvas text scale |
| Live figures in a product | Web or SaaS report infographic | States, period, filters, provenance |

## Archetype contracts

**Statistical chart.** Geometry from the question (bars for categories, lines for ordered time,
scatter for relationships, maps only for spatial evidence, small multiples for consistent
comparison). Zero-based bars, honest line scales, direct labels on key results, uncertainty where
relevant, a dated source. No pie with many categories, no 3D.

**Map or geographic figure.** Used only when location is the evidence. Title, legend, scale or a "not to scale" note; boundary and base-map source, date and licence in the register; boundaries correct for the date shown (districts and counties change); shaded maps use rates or shares against a stated denominator, not raw counts; an equal-area projection when areas are compared across a wide latitude range; labels typeset as real text; a table or list as the text equivalent; sourced and rights-cleared before print at actual size.

**Process flow.** Unique step identifiers in the true order; inputs, transformations, decisions,
outputs and exceptions distinguished; unambiguous arrowheads; no silent crossings; explicit
rejoins; prerequisites, tools, safety warnings, timings, failure states and the final check verified
by an expert.

**Cycle.** Only for a genuinely recurring process. One direction (clockwise by default), a numbered
entry point, every stage once, labels outside crowded rings, feedback only when real and drawn apart
from backward rework; say whether stages repeat identically or evolve.

**Timeline or roadmap.** A true time axis when duration or spacing matters; otherwise labelled as a
sequence. Planned, in progress, completed and delayed states distinct; owners and dependencies on
operational roadmaps; slippage shown, not hidden.

**Comparison.** Criteria first, applied symmetrically with comparable denominators, periods, units
and image sizes; advantages, limits and fit for context; every cell filled or "N/A"; judgements and
scoring basis flagged.

**Hierarchy.** Real reporting or containment; a predictable root position; solid reporting lines
and dotted advisory lines; consistent level depth; an expandable form for deep trees on phones.

**Journey or service blueprint.** Trigger, awareness, evaluation, action, delivery, support and
retention only where they apply; frontstage and backstage separated; pain points tied to research;
hypothesised emotions labelled apart from measured feedback.

**Technical cutaway or isometric map.** Perspective explains spatial relationships without implying
impossible connections or false scale; flows, valves, inputs, outputs, controls and safety
boundaries labelled with consistent line styles; hazardous paths separated; expert-verified.

**Educational or safety poster.** One coherent concept, or clearly partitioned modules; learning
outcome stated; terms defined; familiar to unfamiliar; an actionable takeaway or self-check; no
inferable hazardous shortcut; clinical terms exact; general education kept apart from individual
advice; a qualified reviewer signs.

**Social or mobile card.** Designed for the feed, not a shrunken desktop poster: a strong first
frame, square, portrait and story variants, critical text clear of crops and overlays, proofed on a
real phone after compression, with alt text.

**Web or SaaS report infographic.** A trustworthy data product, not a decorated table: semantic HTML
and accessible SVG, shared tokens with print and PDF, designed empty, loading, partial, error,
restricted and stale states, and period, filters, generation time and provenance always shown.

## Worked cases (handbook Annex M)

| Case | Delivery | Specification |
|---|---|---|
| M.1 Seven-step construction process | Vertical A2 poster | Seven unique numbered stages, arrows alternating across two columns, a cross-section at the bottom, a clear completion state; material layers, units and safety warnings verified |
| M.2 Financial performance | Browser report and PDF | Four KPI summaries, a properly scaled time series, comparison bars, filters, reporting period, a footnote explaining calculations; export keeps data values and sources |
| M.3 Customer lifecycle journey | Web and slide | Discovery to after-sales, each stage with actor, touchpoint, action, pain point and improvement; loop back only for real repeat engagement |
| M.4 Industrial facility process map | A2 landscape and SVG | Isometric zones with unique labels, input and output routes, a readable legend, domain-approved flow topology, no false physical scale |
| M.5 Public-health education | Print and mobile | An illustrated mechanism with accurate terms, cautious evidence, no invented prevalence figures, and a plain-language alternative description |
| M.6 Business comparison | Social and DOCX | Two alternatives on identical criteria, aligned columns, balanced icons, evidence apart from judgement |
| M.7 Roadmap and progress | Web and PPTX | A date-aware timeline with planned and completed status, owners, milestones and visible slippage |
| M.8 Feedback loop | Training slide and wall poster | One clockwise five-stage loop, a numbered entry point, action owners and a final feedback-to-start connection |

For each case, record four sets of decisions in the project file: brief and editorial structure,
visual composition, technical construction, and verification and release. Draft with explicit
dimensions and exact labels, check every numerical or sequential relationship, and keep the
approved version; a reviewer must be able to say why each element exists, how it serves the message
and how it behaves in each delivery format.

## Default scenario pattern

When a team asks for an infographic with a handful of verified facts for a first-time reader:
write the one-sentence takeaway, map each fact to a primary visual and supporting annotations, use a
restrained two-colour system, add a dated source note, and test the mobile and print renditions
separately. If it cannot fit legibly, make a series. Evidence: approved copy sheet, numbered element
inventory, export screenshots, source and calculation register, issue log, signed release checklist.
