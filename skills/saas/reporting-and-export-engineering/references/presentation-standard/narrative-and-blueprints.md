# Narrative and slide blueprints

Use this reference to shape the argument and choose a blueprint for each slide. The handbook's 22
archetypes and 20 blueprints are merged here into one set by communication job. Rule IDs:
[rules-and-gates.md](rules-and-gates.md).

## Task 1: frame the deck (DECK-BRIEF-001, DECK-STORY-001)

Record the audience, presentation mode (live, self-reading, hybrid), decision sought, speaking time,
language and confidence of the evidence. Write a one-sentence thesis. Draft the headlines first: read
them alone, in order; they must make the whole argument.

| Deck type | Sequence |
|---|---|
| Executive review | Decision request, measure summary, drivers, exceptions, options, actions |
| Investor pitch | Opportunity, problem, solution, evidence, business model, market, traction, team, ask |
| Consulting proposal | Client situation, diagnostic, objectives, method, deliverables, timeline, governance, fees, risks |
| Project status | Overall status, completed work, schedule and cost, blockers, forecast, decisions needed |
| Training | Learning outcomes, concept, example, practice, assessment, recap |
| Case study | Context, baseline, intervention, results, limitations, lessons, next step |

Argument spine for persuasive decks: problem, evidence, insight, alternatives, recommendation,
implementation, risk, ask. Within the first three slides the audience knows the problem, why it
matters now and what is being asked. The close restates the recommendation, quantified implications
where evidenced, owner, timing, next action and open questions; never a lone "Thank you".

## Task 2: choose the blueprint by communication job (DECK-LAYOUT-001, DECK-LAYOUT-003)

| Blueprint | Job | Content contract | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover | Subject, context and credibility in three seconds | One assertion headline (30-48 pt), subtitle, presenter, organisation, date, one intentional hero image or motif | Ornamental charts, fake metrics, tiny sponsor logos, text over busy photographs |
| Executive summary | Decision without the full talk | Conclusion headline, 3-5 evidence-backed findings, implication, explicit ask; optional one decisive chart | An agenda disguised as a summary |
| Section divider | Visual reset | Section number, message, subtle image or accent block | Decoration that carries no message |
| Problem and urgency | Whose problem, its scale, the cost of inaction | One quantitative anchor, human or contextual evidence, timeframe, assumptions, source | Invented opportunity cost; causality from correlation |
| Market sizing | Credible TAM, SAM, SOM or segmentation | Nested or stacked model with definitions, geography, period, conversion assumptions, sensitivity, consistent units | Concentric circles with no calculable relationship |
| Solution or product | How the solution resolves the problem | Large screenshot or workflow with 3-4 annotated outcomes tied to the pain points | Every feature at once; a simulated product that does not exist |
| Measure scorecard | Performance at a glance | 3-6 measures with baselines, direction in words, timeframe, interpretation | Unlabelled percentages; status by colour alone |
| Trend analysis | Change over time | Line chart, direct labels, meaningful scale, clear period, annotated inflection | Truncated or unlabelled baselines |
| Composition | Parts of a whole | Sorted bars first; a doughnut only with labelled shares and total | 3D pies; many thin slices |
| Before and after | Measured change | Same measures, common scale, documented intervention, attribution caveat | Different scales on each side |
| Option comparison | Choose between alternatives | Identical criteria in aligned columns, weights and rationale, explicit trade-offs | Cherry-picked categories; unequal scales |
| Process or method | Repeatable sequence and accountability | 4-7 numbered stages, actors, inputs, outputs, deliverable and owner per stage, decision gates, exception route | Arrows without meaning; circular decoration |
| Timeline or roadmap | What happens when and what depends on what | Milestones and dates, dependencies, owner, status, critical path, decision gates, confidence, next review | An impossible schedule; an ambiguous year |
| Project status | Fast, actionable delivery picture | Status with labels, completed and at-risk milestones, variance to plan, dependencies, blockers, owner, decision needed | Activity counts presented as outcomes |
| Risk register or matrix | Make uncertainty actionable | Top risks with likelihood, impact, exposure, mitigation, owner, residual exposure, trigger, due date | Decorative traffic lights; green-only optimism |
| Financial summary | Economics and decision implications | Revenue, cost, gross margin, net contribution, cash effect, period, assumptions; reconciled | Decorative figures; missing denominators |
| Budget or investment request | Funding needed and its use | Total, allocation, phasing, contingency, expected outcomes, currency, taxes, inclusions and exclusions, payment terms, decision requested | Sums that do not reconcile; unnamed currency |
| Results or impact | Measured outcomes with fair comparison | Baseline, endline, period, sample, source, counterfactual limits, explanation | Correlation labelled as proven impact |
| Case study | Credible transformation | Context, challenge, intervention, measured result as the headline, limitation, lesson; approved client evidence | Fabricated testimonials; unsupported attribution |
| Team or capability | Why the team is credible | Roles, relevant experience, contribution, governance, verified photographs and credentials, real reporting lines | Long biographies; tiny portraits; invented awards |
| Quote or testimonial | Voice of a real stakeholder | Attributed, permissioned quote with short context | Any invented endorsement |
| Photo narrative | One message carried by one image | One strong relevant photograph, accessible caption | Image mosaics |
| Geography or coverage | Spatial scope and its significance | Accurate map, legend, dated coverage, labels, operational measures; a table for precise values | Decorative maps with unsupported dots |
| Healthcare or clinical | Service or programme evidence, safely | Real-world imagery, patient pathway, evidence grades, data governance, safety and source details | Invented efficacy, patient data or treatment claims |
| Agriculture or sustainability | Activities linked to measured outcomes | Contextual photographs, supply-chain or ecosystem diagram, baseline, resource use, jobs, environment, measurement plan | Greenwashing; unverified carbon claims |
| Customer journey | Stages, friction and interventions | Swimlane by stage with user goal, action, pain point, evidence, improvement | Ornamental arrows with no evidence |
| Recommendations | What to do next | Ranked actions with expected impact, effort, owner, next checkpoint | Unranked wish lists |
| Recommendation or ask (close) | An unambiguous decision | Proposed action, alternatives, rationale, cost, timeline, accountable owner, amount or decision, contact, immediate next step | A vague "Thank you" |
| Appendix or backup | Support without clutter | Definitions, method, detailed tables, citations, supplementary evidence, linked from the main deck | Presenting dense appendix slides as main slides |

## Task 3: compose every slide

- Title zone at the top; the primary evidence or visual takes 55-70% of the content area; secondary
  explanation takes the rest (DECK-LAYOUT-002).
- Three variants per blueprint: evidence-led white canvas, image-led split canvas, and a dark section
  variant where context supports it.
- Build sequence: draft the assertion headline; identify the primary evidence or story asset; pick a
  native master layout; populate source-backed content; check alignment, contrast and reading order;
  review at projected size and as a static PDF.
- Acceptance: a reader states the message after five seconds; every essential figure is labelled and
  traceable; nothing clipped or overlapping; content editable; theme and footer consistent; usable in
  landscape PDF.

## Task 4: lessons from the reference collection

| Family | Keep | Improve |
|---|---|---|
| Executive and status reporting | Scorecards, status matrices, milestones, short management summaries | Dense tables and small labels that fail on projectors |
| Logistics and corporate decks | Consistent brand colours, modular layouts, process flows, journey diagrams | Repeated template slides and decorative charts |
| Consulting and proposal decks | Clear method, scope, pricing, timelines, problem-to-solution framing | Overdecorated covers, generic claims, tables without decisions |
| Healthcare, sustainability, agriculture | Authentic contextual photography, calm thematic palettes | Stock clichés and unverified clinical claims |
| Infographic libraries | A wide vocabulary of processes, comparisons, hierarchies, maps, roadmaps | Decorative complexity that hides meaning; watermarks |
| Analytical presentations | Large numbers, data-led titles, strong comparisons | Unlabelled percentages, distorted scales, meaningless doughnuts |
| Case studies and impact decks | Challenge, intervention, evidence, outcome storytelling | Correlation presented as causation; unsupported impact |

These are inspiration categories, never instructions to reproduce protected template designs.
