# Tech-Lead Learning Loop

Use this when assigning technical leadership, reviewing a delivery decision, or
turning a team problem into an improvement.

## Role contract

Separate technical, project, and people responsibilities. Name the decision
scope, decision owner, affected stakeholders, escalation path, and the work the
lead must stop doing to create room for leadership. Delegate outcomes and
context, not only tasks.

## Implement, reflect, adjust

1. Define a growth or delivery hypothesis and a visible measure.
2. Implement the smallest change with the team, not around the team.
3. Review delivery evidence, stakeholder feedback, team trust signals, and
   unexpected costs without blame.
4. Adjust the practice, record what changed, and set the next review date.

## Team behaviours to preserve

- communicate decisions and uncertainty transparently;
- build relationships with engineers, managers, product, and adjacent owners;
- use one-to-ones and feedback as learning channels when those inputs are available;
- transfer ownership and document the context needed for independent decisions;
- distinguish a person problem from a system, role-clarity, workload, or process problem.

Do not infer team health from silence or a single retrospective. If the available
early-release material is insufficient, state the missing evidence and use a
qualified coaching plan rather than a judgement.
