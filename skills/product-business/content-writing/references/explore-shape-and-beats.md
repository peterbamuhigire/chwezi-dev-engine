# Explore, Shape, and Beat Writing

Separate discovery from final prose.

1. Explore fragments: collect claims, scenes, examples, objections, evidence, phrases, and questions
   without forcing order. Label source and uncertainty.
2. Shape: choose the reader change, argument or narrative movement, exclusions, and section order.
   Remove fragments that do not serve it.
3. Beats: define what the reader must understand or feel before the next section can work. Ground a
   concept before relying on it later.
4. Draft incrementally: write one beat, test its claim/evidence/transition, then continue. Do not pour
   every fragment into a polished outline.
5. Revise against audience, purpose, factual support, rhythm, repetition, and next action.

Fragments are working material, not publication evidence. Do not fabricate quotes, examples, clients,
or scenes to fill a beat. Label hypothetical material and preserve source traceability.

This reference adapts the fragment, shape, and beat workflows studied in the in-progress writing
skills of Matt Pocock's `mattpocock/skills` repository at commit `3cca18b`.
