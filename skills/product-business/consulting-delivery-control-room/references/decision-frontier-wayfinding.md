# Decision-Frontier Programme Wayfinding

For a long-running programme, keep five linked views: destination/outcome, confirmed path, current
frontier, fog/unknowns, and blocking graph. A session should close one material decision or evidence
gap rather than reopen the whole programme.

At session start, inspect the durable control room and ask: which unblocked decision most changes the
next safe action? Bring only its prerequisites, owner, options, risks, and artefact pointers into
context. At close, update the decision, downstream effects, newly exposed frontier, blockers,
evidence, and next owner.

Do not use journey metaphors in client-facing records when plain programme terms are clearer. The
control artefacts remain decision log, dependency graph, RAID register, deliverable register, and
handoff. Missing stakeholder or approval evidence leaves the branch blocked.

This reference adapts the destination, fog, frontier, and one-decision-session mechanism studied in
Matt Pocock's `mattpocock/skills` repository at commit `3cca18b`.
